BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Good to know.

No account required for the local workflow. Your desktop and mobile records are designed around local-first use. Pairing and two-way LAN sync are manual, rather than automatic cloud services.

Your data matters. Before switching computers or reinstalling, keep independent backups of important research records and exported files.
