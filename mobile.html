01

Quick Note at the bench

实验台旁快速记录

Create a note from Home or the Android widget. Take a photo, import images, add a table, link resources, and finish the record later on desktop.

从首页或 Android 小组件创建笔记，拍照、导入图片、添加表格并关联资源，之后在桌面端完整整理。

02

Protocol, Repeat & calculators

Protocol、Repeat 与实验计算

Start from a synced protocol or reuse a previous note with its linked resources. Insert dilution, molarity, unit-conversion, and PCR master-mix results directly into the note.

从同步的 Protocol 开始，或复用旧笔记及其关联资源；将稀释、摩尔浓度、单位换算和 PCR Master Mix 结果直接写入笔记。

03

Todo, exact Timer & lock screen

Todo、精确计时与锁屏提醒

Schedule experiment tasks and run second-, minute-, or hour-level timers. Android can request exact alarms and keep the nearest active timer visible on the lock screen.

安排实验任务，并运行秒、分钟或小时级计时；Android 可申请精确闹钟，并在锁屏显示最近的活动 Timer。

04

Cell Culture lifecycle

细胞培养全周期追踪

Record thaw, passage, and freeze events with culture history, passage number, frozen-stock details, variants, and the next passage reminder.

记录复苏、传代与冻存，保留培养历史、代数、冻存 Stock、细胞变体和下次传代提醒。

05

Reagent Safety in context

随试剂出现的安全信息

Search and save PubChem-backed reagents while keeping signal words, hazards, precautions, emergency response, storage, and disposal visible.

搜索并保存 PubChem 试剂，同时查看信号词、危险说明、防范、事故响应、储存与废弃处理。

06

Literature & 80/20 Discovery

文献管理与 80/20 发现

Manage folders, import or download legal open-access PDFs, keep reading progress, and discover papers with roughly 80% core-field and 20% adjacent-field recommendations.

管理文件夹、导入或下载合法开放获取 PDF、保留阅读进度，并以约 80% 本领域和 20% 相邻领域发现论文。

07

Traceable public search

可追溯的公共科研搜索

Search genes, proteins, papers, structures, cells, and chemicals through NCBI, UniProt, Europe PMC, RCSB PDB, Cellosaurus, and PubChem.

通过 NCBI、UniProt、Europe PMC、RCSB PDB、Cellosaurus 和 PubChem 查询基因、蛋白、论文、结构、细胞与化合物。

08

Public Search and BYOK AI stay separate

公共搜索与 BYOK AI 保持分离

Public searches never invoke AI. DeepSeek or OpenAI is used only when selected, with the API key kept in SecureStore and notes attached only by the user.

公共搜索不会调用 AI；只有用户选择时才使用 DeepSeek 或 OpenAI，API Key 保存在 SecureStore，笔记也只在用户主动附加时发送。

Desktop ↔ Mobile

Local first. Deliberately synced.

本地优先，由用户主动同步。

Notes, literature, resources, relationships, protocols, and cell culture records stay available locally. Pair by QR code and sync through the lab network—without placing the entire workflow behind a cloud account.

笔记、文献、资源关系、Protocol 与细胞培养记录优先保存在本地。通过二维码配对后在实验室局域网内同步，不必把整个工作流放到云端账号之后。
