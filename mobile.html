Not a smaller desktop. A mobile ELN with its own job.

不是缩小的桌面端，而是承担现场任务的移动 ELN。

01

Quick Notes

快速笔记

Create notes, add images and tables, link cells or reagents, and organize later on desktop.

创建笔记、加入图片与表格、关联细胞或试剂，之后在桌面端完整整理。

02

Cell Culture

细胞培养追踪

Record thaw, passage, and freeze events with passage number, culture history, stock location, and the next passage reminder.

记录复苏、传代与冻存，保留代数、培养历史、冻存位置，并设置下次传代提醒。

03

Reagent Safety

试剂安全

Search PubChem-backed records and keep signal words, hazards, precautions, storage, and disposal visible before use.

搜索 PubChem 资源，并在使用前直接查看信号词、危险说明、防范、储存与废弃处理。

04

Literature

移动文献

Browse saved folders, open PDFs, keep reading progress, and turn useful passages into experiment notes.

浏览文献文件夹、打开 PDF、保留阅读进度，并把有用段落转成实验笔记。

05

Timers & reminders

计时与提醒

Create incubation timers and experiment reminders, including culture-linked next-passage notifications.

设置孵育计时与实验提醒，包括与细胞培养关联的下次传代通知。

06

Public search

公共资源查询

Search genes, proteins, papers, structures, cells, and chemicals from traceable public sources.

从可追溯公共来源查询基因、蛋白、论文、结构、细胞和化合物。

Desktop ↔ Mobile

Local first. Deliberately synced.

本地优先，由用户主动同步。

Notes, literature, resources, relationships, protocols, and cell culture records stay available locally. Pair by QR code and sync through the lab network—without placing the entire workflow behind a cloud account.

笔记、文献、资源关系、Protocol 与细胞培养记录优先保存在本地。通过二维码配对后在实验室局域网内同步，不必把整个工作流放到云端账号之后。
